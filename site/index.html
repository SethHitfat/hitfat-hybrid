FOR GYM OWNERS

The HITFAT
Hybrid Network

Selected gyms across Malaysia, each keeping its own name and its own members — sharing one platform and one national community.

What your club gets

  • Your own club page — name, logo, city and weekly timetable
  • QR check-in that updates each member's streak and score as they scan
  • Attendance for every session, and a flag when a member has stopped coming
  • A coach console for your schedule, members, notes and announcements
  • Your members on the platform for free, in a national community rather than a private app
Apply to join
Applications are reviewed by HITFAT. Not every gym is accepted — the network is built one club at a time.

You keep your brand

A partner club is listed under its own name. HITFAT Hybrid is the line underneath, never a replacement for it.

No lock-in on your members

Your members' accounts are theirs. If they move city, they keep their training history and join the club there.
