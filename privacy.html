HITFAT HYBRID

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026 · hybrid.hitfat.io

HITFAT Hybrid is a training app for hybrid and HYROX athletes. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and who else touches it. It describes what the app actually does — not a template.

The short version. We keep your account details and your training history so your plan and progress follow you between devices. Camera footage from Wall Ball Analysis never leaves your phone. We never see or store your card or banking details, and we do not sell anything about you to anyone.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name and email addressTo create your account and unlock the programs you buy. Supplied by Google when you sign in with Google, or typed by you.
Training data — sessions completed, notes, scores, streaks, race goals, wall ball setsTo show your progress and keep it in sync across devices.
Your height, if you enter it in Wall Ball AnalysisUsed to convert pixels to metres so ball speed and throw height can be calculated. Stored on your device.
Purchase records — order number, program, amount, status, dateTo unlock what you paid for and to support you if a payment goes wrong.
Strava activities and tokens, only if you connect StravaTo import your runs and workouts automatically. You can disconnect at any time in Profile.

What we do not collect

  • Video or images of you. Wall Ball Analysis runs the pose model on your own device. Nothing from your camera is uploaded, transmitted or stored by us.
  • Card or banking details. Payments are handled entirely by Bayarcash. We receive only whether a payment succeeded, its order number and amount.
  • Location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Who processes your data

ServiceRole
SupabaseAccounts, sign-in and the database holding your training data

Each of these holds data only to provide its part of the service.

Data kept on your device

Some information is stored in your browser so the app works offline and starts quickly — your cached training data, your wall ball log, your chosen ball weight and rep target, and your Strava connection. Clearing your browser data removes it from that device; anything already synced to your account returns when you sign in again.

How long we keep it

Your account and training data are kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for support and accounting. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove your personal data and training history, keeping only what we must for payment records.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Strava — Profile → Connected Apps. Stops all activity import immediately.
  • Reset Progress — Profile → Settings. Clears your logged sessions, streak and notes.
  • Sign out — Profile → Settings.
  • Delete your account and data — email us and we will action it.

Children

HITFAT Hybrid is not intended for anyone under 16.
